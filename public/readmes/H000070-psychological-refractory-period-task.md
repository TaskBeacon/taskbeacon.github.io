# Psychological Refractory Period Task (Web)
Browser companion to canonical `T000070-psychological-refractory-period-task`. It preserves five SOAs, balanced 2 x 2 stimulus identities, fixed S2 onset, Task-1 priority, independent RT origins, 20 practice and 160 scored trials. Browser sound/event delivery replaces PsychoPy/hardware triggers.

The notify workflow dispatches `html-task-updated`. `TASKBEACON_ORG_DISPATCH_TOKEN` requires `TaskBeacon/psyflow-web` Contents read/write access and may require organization approval.
